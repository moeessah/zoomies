The Problem

Vet bills shouldn't be a gamble.

Most pet owners skip or delay vet visits because they can't predict the bill. A sick visit, a blood test, a follow-up, it adds up fast. You walk in hoping for $150 and walk out with $800 of guilt and paperwork.

Nearly 66% of pet owners have put off a vet visit over cost fears. In NJ, that number is even higher. That's not a personal failing, it's a broken system.

We built Zoomies to fix that.
